# FATF Recommendation 16 — Wire Transfers (Synthetic summary)

**Body:** Financial Action Task Force (FATF)
**Regulation ID:** REG-FATF-R16
*Synthetic summary for the ARGUS demo. Not an official text.*

## Section 16.1 — Cross-Institution Transparency
Originator and beneficiary information must accompany cross-border and
cross-institution wire transfers so that transfers can be traced end to end.

## Section 16.4 — Layering and Circular Flows
Institutions should monitor for layering, including funds that move through a
chain of institutions and return to or near their origin (circular flows), as
this obscures the source of funds. Such flows often only become visible when
activity is viewed across institutions rather than within a single one.
