## Article 8 - Money Mule and Pass-Through Accounts
Banks shall implement monitoring that identifies accounts used as money mules,
including accounts that receive funds from multiple unrelated parties or from
multiple financial institutions and rapidly consolidate or forward those funds.
Because such networks deliberately span several institutions, monitoring limited
to a single bank's own customers is not sufficient to meet this Article.

## Article 12 - Cash Structuring
Banks shall detect series of cash deposits structured below the applicable cash
transaction reporting threshold, including deposits made on consecutive days by
the same customer, and shall report them to the General Directorate of Financial
Investigation (GDFI) where suspicion arises.

## Article 17 - Sharing of Suspicious Identifiers
Where a bank identifies an identity document, device, or contact detail linked to
suspected financial crime, it shall be able to determine whether the same
identifier is associated with customers at other licensed institutions, using
privacy-preserving mechanisms approved by SAMA, without disclosing customer data
beyond what is necessary.
