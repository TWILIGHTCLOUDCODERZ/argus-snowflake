## Article 15 — Structuring and Smurfing
Financial institutions shall detect and report patterns of multiple cash
deposits made below the cash transaction reporting (CTR) threshold of
AED 55,000 where the pattern indicates an intent to evade reporting. Series of
deposits by the same customer aggregating above the threshold within a short
window must be escalated and filed.

## Article 22 — Customer Identity Integrity
A customer's identity must be unique and verified at onboarding. Reuse of the
same national identity document, device, or contact number to open accounts —
particularly across multiple institutions — is a red flag for synthetic-identity
fraud and must be investigated.

## Article 31 — Money Mule Networks
Institutions must monitor for money mule activity, including rapid pass-through
of funds, fan-in/fan-out account structures, and accounts that consolidate
inflows from many unrelated parties before onward transfer.
