# CBB Rulebook - Financial Crime Module (FC) (Synthetic)

**Regulator:** Central Bank of Bahrain (CBB)
**Regulation ID:** REG-CBB-FC-2024
**Effective:** 2024-03-01
*Synthetic document for the ARGUS demo. Not the real CBB Rulebook.*

## FC-1.5 - Duplicate Identities Across Licensees
Licensees, including finance companies (NBFCs), must take reasonable steps to
detect when the same identity document or device is used to open accounts or
facilities at more than one licensee, as this is a key indicator of
synthetic-identity and first-party fraud. Checks confined to the licensee's own
customer base do not satisfy this requirement.

## FC-4.2 - Cross-Border Corridor Monitoring
Licensees must apply enhanced monitoring to transfers moving through GCC
cross-border corridors, including chains of transfers across several institutions
and flows that return to, or close to, their point of origin.

## FC-6.1 - Timely Suspicious Transaction Reporting
Where a licensee knows or suspects that a transaction involves the proceeds of
crime, it must file a Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) with the Financial
Intelligence Directorate within three business days of forming that suspicion,
with a narrative that cites the supporting transactions.
